## 1. Operational Command Center Principles

TRINETRA delivers a unified operational command and governance architecture designed to give mine safety leadership and statutory regulators complete spatial, predictive, and auditable oversight of underground coal mine assets.

### 10-Second Executive Rule:
Within 10 seconds of opening the Command Dashboard, an executive or judge must immediately grasp:
1. **Current Risk State**: Quantitative composite index (0–100) and severity rating.
2. **Critical Breaches**: Any active gas escalations, roof convergence alarms, or ventilation anomalies.
3. **Statutory Non-Compliance**: DGMS violation notices, overdue corrective actions, and SLA breaches.
4. **Predictive Horizon**: 30-minute forward escalation forecasts generated by machine learning.
5. **System Operational Status**: Health of integrations, telemetry pipelines, and field sync queues.

## 4. Responsible AI & Data Provenance Standards

1. **Evidence Grounding**: AI Copilot responses must always cite verifiable backend entity references (`Sensor CH4-042`, `DGMS Regulation 115`, `Anomaly AN-1024`).
2. **Transparent ML Limitations**: Clearly display model version (`TRINETRA-GBM-v2.0`), forecast horizon (`+30 Minutes`), and empirical dataset provenance (`TRAINING DATA: SIMULATED / DEMO`).
3. **Human-in-the-Loop**: All automated suggestions (incident dispatch, corrective action approval) require explicit human sign-off with dual-factor verification.
4. **Offline Resilience**: Field operation forms clearly display synchronization states (`OFFLINE (3 QUEUED)` / `ONLINE (SYNC COMPLETE)`).
